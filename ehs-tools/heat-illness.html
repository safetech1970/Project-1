Close observation for 14 days

New employees, and anyone newly assigned to a high-heat area, must be closely watched by a supervisor or designee for their first 14 days. During a heat wave, closely observe every worker.

Under observation

Heat stroke is a medical emergency. Call 911.

Do not leave the worker alone. Do not send a sick worker home or to a car without someone watching them.

Heat exhaustion

    Move to shade or a cool area, loosen clothing, give small sips of cool water, cool with wet cloths. If not better in 15 minutes or symptoms worsen, call 911.

    Heat stroke

      Call 911 now. Cool the worker fast: ice packs at neck, armpits and groin, soak clothing with cool water, fan them. Do not give fluids if they are confused or not fully alert.
