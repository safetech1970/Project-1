Privacy Policy — EHS Field Tools

Last updated: November 2026 · Beta

What we collect

Nothing. EHS Field Tools has no accounts and no servers that receive your data. Everything you enter — inspections, photos, signatures, names, training records — is stored only in your browser on your own device.

AI features (optional)

If you add your own Anthropic API key, the text and photos you choose to analyze are sent directly from your device to Anthropic to produce the AI result, under your own Anthropic account and Anthropic's terms. Your key is stored only on your device. If you don't add a key, nothing is sent.

Sharing and exports

PDFs, spreadsheets and backups are created on your device. They leave it only when you choose to share, email or upload them.

Deleting your data

Clear the site's data in your browser settings, or use the delete and erase options inside each tool. Uninstalling the app removes its data.

Children

The tools are intended for workplace safety professionals and are not directed to children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/safetech1970/Project-1.

Resumen en español
